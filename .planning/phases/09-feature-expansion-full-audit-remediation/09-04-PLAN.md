<objective>
Harden the read side and session lifecycle: anonymous readers get a fixed
spoiler boundary (order 1) instead of trusting a client-chosen
`visible_until_order`; candidate reads require a resolved server-side
boundary; the fail-closed visibility policy stops turning a None-valued order
into a 500; and sessions become collision-proof with a background sweep that
replaces the write-on-every-read slide. This closes PROBLEMS #9, #12, #13, #32,
#37 — the spoiler-safety-of-the-read-path and unbounded-DB-growth findings.

Purpose: The spoiler guarantee must not depend on the client's honesty, and
session storage must not grow without bound or fail under same-second logins.
Output: Server-side boundary resolution for anonymous/candidate reads, a
422-on-None policy edge, and uuid4 + swept sessions, all test-locked.
</objective>
