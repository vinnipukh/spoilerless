<objective>
Replace Plan 01's minimal unified DetailPanel with the full D-06/D-07 branch: nodes and claim-backed narrative edges (edges carrying a non-null `claim_id`) open the Overview/Claims/Evidence tabbed `Sheet` defined in 02-UI-SPEC.md; structural edges (`PART_OF`/`PRECEDES`, `claim_id` is null) open a distinct, simpler `StructuralEdgeCard` with no tabs, signaling "not a narrative claim" rather than showing empty/disabled claim/evidence tabs.

This is horizontal expansion on top of the Plan 01 tracer - `GraphCanvas`'s `onSelect` callback contract is unchanged; only what `App.tsx` renders in response to a selection changes, and `DetailPanel` itself gains real tab content instead of a flat label dump.

Purpose: satisfy UI-04's requirement that node and edge/claim detail views "explain relationships and display linked source/evidence episode locators."
Output: A fully-tabbed detail experience for nodes/claims and a distinct minimal card for structural scaffold edges, both covered by component tests.
</objective>
